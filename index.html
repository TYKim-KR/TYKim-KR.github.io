ty-assistant

A personal Telegram assistant, self-hosted and used by a single person.

What it is

ty-assistant is a private assistant that its owner talks to through Telegram. It runs continuously on the owner's own machine as a Docker container. It is not a product, it is not offered to other users, and there is no sign-up.

What it does

  • Answers questions and runs small tasks sent as Telegram messages.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar (read-only) to answer schedule questions and to send a daily briefing.
  • Reads and writes notes in the owner's own local knowledge folder.

Google account access

The app requests only the scopes it needs to do the above:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly — read calendar events. It cannot create, edit, or delete anything.
  • openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — identify which Google account was connected.

Access is granted by the account owner and can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account permissions.
